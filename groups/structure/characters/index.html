Check your own character

Computed a character by hand? Type it in below and have it checked. Columns are the conjugacy classes of \(G\), with the class size underneath the representative.

Entries may be any complex expression: -1, 1/2, 2i, 1-i, sqrt5, (1+sqrt5)/2, 2cos(2pi/5), e^(2pi i/3). The shorthands w or omega (= \(e^{2\pi i/3}\)) and phi (= \((1+\sqrt5)/2\)) are recognised, as are π, √, ω and φ.
